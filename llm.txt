# Build to Understand Studio

> Build to Understand Studio creates educational games and interactive longreads for museums, universities and media.

The studio researches a subject, develops three interactive approaches, and finishes the chosen idea for the audience and channel. The seven home-page stories use different visual styles but describe the same studio and services. The Map version at the site root is the main version.

## Main pages

- [Home and how we work](https://buildtounderstand.com/): Studio introduction, process, and project enquiry form.
- [Cases](https://buildtounderstand.com/cases.html): Selected interactive philosophy, neuroscience, decision-making, and history projects.
- [Team](https://buildtounderstand.com/team.html): People and collaboration approach.
